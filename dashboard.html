Fix 1 · do today

Delete the placeholder text

10 minutes

Where: Wix dashboard → Bookings → Services → "Free 20-minute Consultation" → Description field. Paste this:

Service description
This free 20-minute video call is a no-pressure chance to see if we're a good fit. Tell me what's bringing you to counselling, ask anything about my approach, and get a feel for how I work. Sessions are online across BC, offered in English, Bengali, and Hindi. Regular sessions are $140; low-cost spots are $75.
Fix 2

Create or claim your Google Business Profile

30 minutes

Where: business.google.com → add "Sentient Counselling," address 201-3701 E Hastings St, Burnaby, category "Counsellor."

This is the single biggest reason competitors show up on the map and in AI Overviews for "counsellor near me" while you don't. If you already have one, tell me and I'll audit it next week instead.

Fix 3

Get listed on southasiantherapists.org

20 minutes

Where: southasiantherapists.org → "Join as a Therapist."

Their BC page ranks at the top for your money keyword and gets cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity. Reuse your Healing in Colour bio word-for-word so the facts match everywhere.

Fix 4

Fix the Burnaby / Vancouver split

15 minutes

Where: Wix → Settings → SEO → homepage title tag. Change it to:

Homepage title tag
South Asian Therapist in Burnaby & Vancouver | Sentient Counselling

You serve both, but your address is Burnaby, claiming only Vancouver means you rank strongly in neither.

Fix 5

Fix Crunchbase

5 minutes

Where: crunchbase.com → claim your profile → remove "EMDR" unless you actually offer it.

One consistent story across every listing is what makes AI models confidently recommend you.
